Why Everything Deduper?
Designed for transparency, predictability and safety.

Most duplicate file tools focus on deleting files as quickly as possible. Everything Deduper takes a different approach. Audit your files, review the results, decide which version should be retained, and verify everything before making permanent changes.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does Everything Deduper automatically delete files?
No. Duplicates are copied to a separate location for review.
Can I scan multiple folders?
Yes. Version 1.5 supports multi‑folder audits.
What are Keeper Rules?
Keeper Rules allow preferred folders to be prioritised when duplicates are processed.
Is Everything Deduper safe?
Everything Deduper is built around review, verification and transparency.
